# Instructions
### Since our data set is too big to be stored in GitHub, here are instructions on how to find the dataset we used using the link below

## Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/extralime/20000-boardgames-dataset/data 

## Step 1: Click the Link
After you click the link, you should be brought to a Kaggle.com page that contains our dataset called ***20,000 Boardgames Dataset***. Your screen should look like this: 

<img width="1277" height="639" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-13 163321" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/23d43cad-0504-4282-b74a-000eec2ccf5f" />


## Step 2: Create a Kaggle Account
In order to view and download this dataset, Kaggle requires you to make an account with them first. If you already have a Kaggle account, log in and you can skip this step. 
If you need to make an account, begin by clicking the Sign Up button on the top right of the screen shown before. Next, register a valid Gmail or other email by clicking the respective 
*Register with Google* or *Register with Email* button. To complete registration, provide Kaggle with your displayed name, click *Next*, and hit *I Agree* to their Privacy Policy and Terms 
of Use. 

## Step 3: Download the Dataset
The download button for this dataset can be found to the far right after the file name and file size: **boardgames1.csv (32.78 MB)**
